## Is this made by Anthropic?

No. ClaudeBar is an independent project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Anthropic.

## Does ClaudeBar prevent account bans?

No. ClaudeBar controls managed local egress, local directory isolation, and pre-launch checks, but it cannot control inherent proxy-node reputation, same-engine browser fingerprint overlap, payment cards and Stripe Radar, email/phone linkage, Persona/Yoti verification, or Sift's cross-merchant reputation graph. See [security boundaries](/ClaudeBar-docs/concepts/security/).

## Why shouldn't I use multiple Chrome profiles for different accounts, and why doesn't ClaudeBar spoof fingerprints?

Multiple profiles or sandboxes inside the same Chrome installation isolate cookies only; roughly 74 of 79 observed Sift hardware and rendering fields remain unchanged, exposing accounts to both graph contagion and active `users_per_device_id` scoring. ClaudeBar also intentionally refuses JS fingerprint spoofing and does not block `s-cdn.anthropic.com`, because tampering artifacts and missing risk beacons create stronger anomalies. Follow the isolation ladder (one machine per account > isolated CLI > one browser engine per Web account) in [multi-account isolation and risk boundaries](/ClaudeBar-docs/guides/multi-account-isolation/).

## Is it a general AI-agent workspace?

No. Its core documented workflows concern managed network egress, session isolation, diagnostics, and recovery on macOS.

## Does it protect every application automatically?

No such guarantee is established here. Understand the managed launch path and the separate deployment requirements of optional system controls. Read [security boundaries](/ClaudeBar-docs/concepts/security/).

## Where do the product screenshots come from?

The [illustrated guide](/ClaudeBar-docs/concepts/visual-guide/) includes actual SwiftUI views rendered by the application's existing screenshot tests. Network states, scores, timings, and the entry names on Launchpad all use fixed sample data. These images explain the interface and do not establish live network or account behavior. Mechanism diagrams and the interactive gate model are labeled teaching aids.

## Why can I not download the app here?

The source is private and this site is not a binary distribution channel. See [installation and access](/ClaudeBar-docs/start/installation/).

## Where is the roadmap?

Internal engineering plans are not automatically public commitments. This edition publishes implemented behavior and explicit gaps rather than a speculative roadmap. The [baseline page](/ClaudeBar-docs/project/status/) tracks unverified areas.

## What is the license?

No public application license was identified in the reviewed baseline. Publicly readable docs do not grant software rights; confirm use and distribution terms with the maintainer.
