## Is this made by Anthropic?

No. ClaudeBar is an independent project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Anthropic.

## Is it a general AI-agent workspace?

No. Its core documented workflows concern managed network egress, session isolation, diagnostics, and recovery on macOS.

## Does it protect every application automatically?

No such guarantee is established here. Understand the managed launch path and the separate deployment requirements of optional system controls. Read [security boundaries](/ClaudeBar-docs/concepts/security/).

## Why are there no product screenshots?

No sanitized, verified screenshot was selected for this documentation release. Diagrams explain behavior and are labeled as illustrations or simulations. They do not represent live app observations.

## Why can I not download the app here?

The source is private and this site is not a binary distribution channel. See [installation and access](/ClaudeBar-docs/start/installation/).

## Where is the roadmap?

Internal engineering plans are not automatically public commitments. This edition publishes implemented behavior and explicit gaps rather than a speculative roadmap. The [baseline page](/ClaudeBar-docs/project/status/) tracks unverified areas.

## What is the license?

No public application license was identified in the reviewed source baseline. Public visibility of documentation does not by itself grant a software license. These docs do not assert MIT, Apache, or another license on the owner's behalf. Contact the maintainer for permitted use and distribution terms.
